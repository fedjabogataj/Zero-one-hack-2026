# Archived experiment results

This directory contains snapshot archives of representative training runs,
generated by `scripts/archive_run.sh`. Each subdirectory holds the per-task
metrics JSON, the submission CSVs from that model, and a per-run README.

**The final submitted model is `transformer_5000_bge-r2`.** Its archive
lives at [`transformer_5000_bge-r2/`](./transformer_5000_bge-r2/). The
other entries are kept here as the comparison baselines that
[`REPORT.md`](../REPORT.md) references.

## What's here vs. what's NOT

| Run | Encoder | Role | Why it's archived |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[`transformer_5000_bge-r2/`](./transformer_5000_bge-r2/)** | BGE-base | **FINAL SUBMISSION** | Picked from the L9 sweep as the Pareto winner under the official scorer (best Top-1 + best MRR + best NED among non-overfit candidates) |
| [`transformer_5000/`](./transformer_5000/) | MiniLM | MiniLM baseline | Comparison point — what the same architecture looks like with the smaller encoder |
| [`transformer_25000/`](./transformer_25000/) | MiniLM | Scaling reference | Shows the scaling ceiling at 25k synthetic sequences |
| [`ngram_o3/`](./ngram_o3/) | — | Statistical baseline | Family-conditioned n-gram (order 3); the floor that every neural approach has to beat |

Not archived (intentionally):

* **The other 8 BGE sweep runs** (r0, r1, r3–r8). Their numbers appear in the
  L9 table in [`REPORT.md`](../REPORT.md) but the per-run archives aren't
  duplicated here — the chosen winner (`bge-r2`) is what matters for the
  submission. The full evaluation results for every run live in
  `outputs/reports/transformer_5000_bge-r*/` on the training cluster and
  in the wandb project (see below).
* **The 9 MiniLM sweep runs** — same reasoning. The L9 was an experiment;
  we kept the baseline `transformer_5000` for comparison.
* **The un-swept `transformer_5000_bge` run** (default hyperparameters).
  Scored Top-1 = 0.7672 in-distribution, suspected of overfit to synthetic
  patterns, deliberately not submitted. See "Why not the obvious pick"
  in [`REPORT.md`](../REPORT.md).
* **Model checkpoints (`.pt`)** — too large for GitHub's 100 MB/file limit.
  Reproduce with the commands in `REPORT.md`; wandb has the loss curves.

## Cross-experiment comparison — headline metrics

Numbers below are from the **organizer's official scorer**
(`official_eval/eval_metrics.py`), not our internal `metrics.py`. The two
disagree only on Task 2 token / block accuracy by ~4-5 percentage points
(uniform shift, doesn't change ranking); everything else matches exactly.

| run | Encoder | Top-1 ↑ | MRR ↑ | NED ↓ | Block acc ↑ | F1 ↑ | ROC-AUC ↑ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **transformer_5000_bge-r2** | **BGE-base** | **0.7125** | **0.8540** | **0.2126** | 0.6988 | 1.0000 | 0.8773 |
| transformer_5000 | MiniLM | 0.7090 | 0.8523 | 0.2166 | 0.6984 | 1.0000 | 0.7940 |
| transformer_25000 | MiniLM | 0.7027 | 0.8489 | 0.2188 | 0.6927 | 1.0000 | 0.8500 |
| ngram_o3 | — | 0.6843 | 0.8370 | 0.5621 | 0.6235 | 1.0000 | 0.5150 |

**Reads at a glance:**

1. **bge-r2 leads on Tasks 1 and 2** (best Top-1, MRR, NED) — the BGE-base
   token init beats the MiniLM equivalent at the same architecture.
2. **Every transformer ties everyone on F1** because the hybrid anomaly
   strategy uses the symbolic rule oracle for `IS_VALID`. The honest
   anomaly signal is **ROC-AUC** on the model's own confidence:
   - n-gram: 0.515 (random)
   - bge-r2: 0.877
3. **MiniLM scaling tops out around 25k** (Top-1 plateaus 0.70-0.71;
   ROC-AUC keeps climbing → 0.85). bge-r2 at 5k outperforms it without
   needing more data.

## To regenerate one of these archives

```bash
./scripts/archive_run.sh outputs/models/transformer_5000_bge-r2.pt
# → results/transformer_5000_bge-r2/{README.md, metrics/, submissions/}
```

The script reads the matching prediction CSVs from `outputs/submissions/`,
the metrics JSONs from `outputs/reports/`, and the model artifact from
`outputs/models/`. If the `.best.pt` sibling exists (it should, after
training), `archive_run.sh` automatically uses the best-val-loss weights.
